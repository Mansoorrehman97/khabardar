Khabardar

Flood warning on every phone, in every language, before the water comes.

1. WATCH — live rain and river forecasts plus NASA satellite flood maps, rain and soil wetness for 58 flood-prone places across Pakistan.
2. WARN — every phone in the danger zone rings with a siren and a voice message in Urdu or English; keypad phones get an SMS.
3. HELP — people tap “I'm safe” or “I need help”; the control room sees who needs rescue, sends the nearest team, and the phone is told help is on the way.
Open the control room (laptop / projector)

Phones join by scanning the QR code shown on the control room screen. Dummy phones are built in for a demo without real handsets.

Source code and documentation on GitHub

Hackathon demo · Capital Youth Expo 2026. Alerts marked DEMO are tests. Data: Open-Meteo & GloFAS (Copernicus), NASA GIBS (MODIS flood, GPM IMERG, SMAP, VIIRS) and NASA EONET. Real-time relay: ntfy.sh online, or the laptop itself in the local edition.
