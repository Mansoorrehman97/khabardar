Khabardar

Flood warning on every phone, in every language, before the water comes.

1. WATCH — live rain and river data for each village.
2. WARN — every phone in the danger zone rings with a siren and a voice message in Urdu, Pashto, Sindhi or English.
3. HELP — people tap “I'm safe” or “I need help”, and the control room sees who needs rescue.
Open the control room (laptop / projector)

Phones join by scanning the QR code shown on the control room screen.

Hackathon demo · Capital Youth Expo 2026. Alerts marked DEMO are tests. Live data: Open-Meteo & GloFAS (Copernicus). Real-time relay: ntfy.sh.
